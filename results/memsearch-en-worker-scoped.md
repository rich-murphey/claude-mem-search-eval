# memsearch-en through claude-mem's worker search (scoped)

memsearch-memory-retrieval, English (machine-translated from Chinese) (zc277584121/memsearch-memory-retrieval @ dbbdc710, MIT); queries and labels generated by gpt-4o-mini. 2172 queries; each query searches its claude-mem project; a document is one observation whose facts are its parts; top 10 from SearchManager.search; 95% intervals from 2000 bootstrap resamples over queries.

memsearch answers "does it help in our domain": Claude Code developer memory, Chinese and English, labels generated by gpt-4o-mini. Its recall-based metrics compare models; its P@5 and F1@5 are capped by single-answer queries and are not absolute measures.

Best treated as a measurement of relative accuracy between models, given the label errors discussed in the README ("Caveat on absolute accuracy").
